Privacy policy

Updated October 10, 2026

Who is responsible

The Math Wolf is a Wolf Academy game published by Nadav9. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email nadavwolfsonw@gmail.com. Never send a password.

Game and account data

Guest progress, lives, settings and recovery copies stay on your device. Optional signed-in services use Base44 and Google authentication and store your name, email, account identifier, profile and private progress snapshots for account access and recovery. Custom gameplay analytics are disabled.

Earlier versions, including Android 1.0.10 and below, used Base44's automatic usage measurement, which may send session identifiers, page addresses and timing information to Base44. Android 1.0.11 and later disable this automatic measurement and skip account checks for guests. This change does not delete data previously processed by Base44. Contact us for privacy or deletion requests.

Optional video ads

Android builds with optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob. Its SDK may collect and share IP-based approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Requests use child-directed, under-age and non-personalized settings; advertising-ID permissions are removed. Internal tests use Google test ads. Ad-free builds omit this SDK.

Google privacy policy

Choices and safeguards

Videos are optional. Players are never required to buy anything; lives refill over time, and Parent Area can limit video rewards. There are no purchases, chat, public profiles, or precise-location, contacts, camera or microphone collection. We do not sell personal data. Service connections use encrypted HTTPS/TLS.

Retention and deletion

Device data remains until cleared. Signed-in profiles and recovery snapshots are retained while the account is active. Settings → Privacy & account provides export and deletion. A successful request removes game data and marks the Base44 sign-in identity for separate closure; its removal date is not yet confirmed. Contact us for status. Google processes SDK data under its own policy.
