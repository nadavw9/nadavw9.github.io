Account and data deletion

Updated October 15, 2026

Request deletion

For The Math Wolf, email nadavwolfsonw@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and ask to delete your account or specific game data. You do not need to reinstall the app. Never send a password or verification code.

If you can sign in, you can also open the game's deletion screen and follow Settings → Privacy & account → Delete account.

What the in-game deletion removes

A successful confirmed request removes the game's cloud profile, progress snapshots, world-ranking entry, verified lesson receipts and account association, and clears local game progress on that device. Deletion cannot be undone. Data on another offline device may remain until removed there.

Remaining records and timing

The Base44 sign-in identity is marked for separate closure; its removal date is not yet confirmed. Automatic usage information processed by earlier versions is not erased by an app update. Contact us to request deletion or check its status; no fixed deletion deadline is promised. Google processes advertising SDK data under its privacy policy.
